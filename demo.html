Demo & beta

Yield Lens is in beta ahead of its Chrome Web Store launch. Watch the demo, or install the beta build yourself.

Demo video (1 minute)

The demo uses sample listing pages. On real Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove and Domain listings the panel works the same way. The last scene opens the real Dodo Payments checkout, in test mode.

Install the beta

Download beta (v1.0.0, 74 KB)

  1. Unzip the downloaded file into a folder.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Open a for-sale listing on Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove or Domain. The Yield Lens panel appears at the bottom right.

Payments in the beta are in test mode. Pro can be tried with the built-in 7-day free trial. In the test checkout you can use the card 4242 4242 4242 4242 with any future expiry date and any CVC. No real charge is made.

What it does

  • Free: cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, gross and net yield, break-even rent, mortgage payment and purchase taxes (UK SDLT/LBTT/LTT, Australian transfer duty by state, US closing costs and property tax by state) on supported listings.
  • Pro ($6.99/month incl. tax): save listings, multiple scenarios per property, a comparison page and CSV export.
  • Licensing: after checkout, the license key from Dodo Payments activates automatically via our activation page. It can also be pasted in the extension settings.
