The demo uses sample listing pages. On real Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove and Domain listings the panel works the same way. The last scene shows the Dodo Payments checkout (recorded in test mode).

Install

Add to Chrome, it's free

Then open a for-sale listing on Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove or Domain. The Yield Lens panel appears at the bottom right.

What it does

  • Free: cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, gross and net yield, break-even rent, mortgage payment and purchase taxes (UK SDLT/LBTT/LTT, Australian transfer duty by state, US closing costs and property tax by state) on supported listings.
  • Pro ($6.99/month incl. tax): save listings, multiple scenarios per property, a comparison page and CSV export.
  • Licensing: after checkout, the license key from Dodo Payments activates automatically via our activation page. It can also be pasted in the extension settings.
