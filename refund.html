Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026

Free trial

Yield Lens Pro includes a one-time 7-day free trial that starts inside the extension. No payment details are required, and you are never charged for the trial.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with Yield Lens Pro, you can ask for a full refund of your first subscription payment within 14 days of that payment, for any reason.

Later payments

Renewal payments after the first one are generally not refunded. You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals. If you were charged in error, for example a duplicate charge, contact us and we will put it right.

Cancelling

  • Cancel anytime from the subscription management link in your purchase email, or by contacting us.
  • After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for. It then stops, and you are not charged again.
  • The free calculator panel keeps working after cancellation.

How to request a refund

Email kyc.com.infomail@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout, and your order or receipt number if you have it. Refunds are processed by our merchant of record, Dodo Payments, to your original payment method. They usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank. When a refund is issued, the Pro license for that purchase is deactivated.

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer laws of your country.
