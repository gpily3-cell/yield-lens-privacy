Know a rental's numbers before you fall for the photos.

Yield Lens is a Chrome extension. It shows cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return and stamp duty right on Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove and Domain listings, for the US, UK and Australia.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store See pricing

Instant investment metrics

Monthly cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, gross and net yield, mortgage payment and total cash needed, all calculated from the listing you are viewing.

Local tax rules built in

UK SDLT with additional-property and non-resident rates, Australian transfer duty with foreign-buyer surcharges, and US property tax by state.

Every input adjustable

Change price, rent, rate, deposit, vacancy, management or maintenance and every figure updates live. Set your own defaults per country.

FAQ

Which sites does it work on?

Zillow and Redfin (US), Rightmove (UK) and Domain (Australia), on for-sale listing pages.


Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Listing data is processed only in your browser. Pro checks your license key with our payment provider. See the privacy policy.


How do I activate Pro after paying?

After checkout you're returned to our activation page, which turns Pro on automatically. Your license key is also emailed to you, and you can paste it in the extension's settings.


Is this financial advice?

No. All figures are estimates based on the listing and your inputs. Always verify with qualified professionals.
