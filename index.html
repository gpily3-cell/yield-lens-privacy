Yield Lens Privacy Policy

Effective: 4 November 2026

Yield Lens is a Chrome extension that shows rental-investment estimates (cash flow, cap rate, yields, stamp duty) on property listings you are viewing. This policy explains what data the extension handles. In short: your browsing and property data stay in your browser. We do not run servers that receive it, and we never sell data.

1. Listing pages you view

On supported listing pages (Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove, Domain), the extension reads listing details already on the page, such as price, address, bedrooms, and the site's rent estimate. It does this locally in your browser to calculate the figures in the panel. This information is not sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension does not run on any other websites.

3. Payments and licenses (Dodo Payments)

Pro subscriptions are sold by Dodo Payments, which acts as the merchant of record and handles checkout, payment processing, taxes, and receipts.

  • The 7-day free trial starts inside the extension. It needs no email address or card, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • When you subscribe, you enter your email address and payment details on Dodo Payments' checkout pages. The extension never sees your card details. Dodo Payments emails you a license key.
  • To activate and periodically check Pro, the extension sends only your license key and a random installation label to Dodo Payments' license service. No browsing or listing data is sent.
  • After checkout you may be redirected to our activation page, with your license key in the link. The page shows the key and passes it to the extension in your browser. The page does not store or send the key elsewhere.
  • The developer can see your email address and subscription status in the Dodo Payments dashboard. These are used only to provide and support your subscription.

Please see the Dodo Payments privacy policy.

4. What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No selling or transferring of user data to third parties, except as needed to process payments as described above.
  • No use of data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose, and no use for credit-worthiness or lending decisions.

5. Estimates, not advice

All figures are estimates based on the listing and your inputs, and are not financial, tax or legal advice. Yield Lens is not affiliated with or endorsed by Zillow, Redfin, Rightmove or Domain.
